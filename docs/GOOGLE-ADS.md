# Google Ads readiness

Configure GA4/GTM only after privacy review and a compliant consent choice. Events include `click_whatsapp`, `click_phone`, `click_email`, `language_change`, `partner_referral_visit`, `cta_services`, and `cta_tourist_help`. Create GA4 key events for phone and WhatsApp, link GA4 to Google Ads, import the key events, and verify them in Tag Assistant/DebugView before spending. Never send contact or medical details.

Use campaign-specific Florence landing pages and UTMs such as `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=tourist_florence&utm_content=hotel_help`. Suggested campaign themes are tourist assistance, nursing/home or hotel care, and home physiotherapy. Keep ad-group language natural and aligned to the unique page copy. Attribution persists supported UTMs and `gclid`; only the referral code, never full campaign data, enters the WhatsApp message.
