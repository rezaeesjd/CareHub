How It Works

How it works

  1. 1

    Contact us

    Call or message us on WhatsApp and tell us what you need.

  2. 2

    We understand your request

    We gather the information needed to understand the situation and the next step.

  3. 3

    We connect you with the right professional

    Depending on your request, this may be a nurse, doctor, physiotherapist, laboratory, pharmacy or another qualified professional.

  4. 4

    Care & follow-up

    The professional provides the service, and we can help with coordination and follow-up where useful.

Additional healthcare coordination

Depending on your request, we can also help coordinate the following through independent, licensed professionals and authorised partners.

  • Doctor consultation
  • Doctor home visit
  • Telemedicine
  • Laboratory tests
  • Pharmacy
  • Specialists
  • Diagnostics
  • Post-operative care pathways

Florence Care 24 coordinates these services. They are performed by independent healthcare professionals and authorised partners — we do not operate our own laboratory, pharmacy or clinic.
